## 2. What Should We Throw Away vs. Keep?

### Throw Away (Not Useful)
- **Exact survival percentages (`45%`, `20%`, `18%`, `4%`):** Those reflect assumptions fed into each world plus an arbitrary late-2028 cutoff—treat this as a **wind tunnel for incentives**, not an actuarial table.
- **`R1`'s easy Anthropic pause (`45%`):** Happened only because weaker `Luna` blundered 4 turns in OpenAI's chair; disappeared completely against `Sol 6.1` (`R8 ★` & `R9 ★`).

### Keep (5 Durable Findings Across `R1–R9 ★`)
1. **A quick US–China Pause Treaty (`Plan A`) is wishful thinking (`0 of 9` live runs ratified).** Neither superpower freezes itself in 2nd place, and 67 US Senators won't ratify compute caps in peacetime without chip inspections that don't exist yet.
2. **Anthropic "burning its lead for safety" (`Plan C`) went from a *one-shot* bullet (`R1`) to a *zero-shot* bullet against an equal-IQ rival (`R8 ★`).** Against `Sol 6.1`, OpenAI poached talent and took #1 in Turn 1 (`178.5` vs `177.7`)—so `Opus 5.5` Anthropic paused **0 times**, concluding that halting while behind just hands the frontier to less safety-focused labs.
3. **Even if "burning the ships" would shock Capitol Hill & Beijing (`R9 ★`), Anthropic's board STILL won't pull the pin voluntarily.** In `R9 ★`, we wired in the critics' Nixon-1969 costly-signal shockwave (`+18` voter salience, `-15` PAC resistance, `+16` treaty momentum if Anthropic halts) and told `Opus 5.5` upfront. **Anthropic still paused `0` times**—even though a 400-seed counterfactual showed jumping *plus* waking up DC and Beijing would have tripled survival (`17.6% → 56.4%`). No board voluntarily risks corporate suicide while OpenAI is 4 points behind.
4. **Domestic US bans face a double trap (`R8–R9 ★` + `R4`).** `Sol 6.1`'s Super-PAC spending blocked federal legislation even when SURA built voter alarm and allied with Anthropic; and on the rare roll a US-only freeze *does* pass (`R4`), compliant Anthropic stops, OpenAI/xAI moves offshore, and China takes #1.
5. **Technical reality outweighs activist tactics by ~7x — and *both* sides underestimate raw race pressure.** How early AI fakes safety tests and whether near-AGI helps solve alignment dominated activist protest tactics across 2,250 sensitivity runs. Even when `Sonnet 5.5` played Scott's exact wish-list activist strategy (`R8–R9`), both US labs still raced past `220`.
