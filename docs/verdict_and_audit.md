## 2. Did We Get Any Useful Results? (Updated After `R8 ★`)

### Throw Away (Not Useful)
- **Exact survival percentages (`45%`, `20%`, `4%`):** Those reflect assumptions we fed each world plus an arbitrary late-2028 cutoff.
- **First-draft `v1` treaty (`87%`) & `R1`'s easy Anthropic pause (`45%`):** `v1`'s treaty came from code bugs; `R1`'s safety pause happened only because weak `Luna` blundered in OpenAI's chair.

### Keep (4 Core Findings — How They Held Up After `R8 ★`)
1. **A quick US–China Pause Treaty (`Plan A`) is wishful thinking — *Held 100% (`0 of 8 runs`)*.** Neither superpower freezes itself in 2nd place, and 67 US Senators won't ratify without chip inspections that don't exist yet.
2. **Anthropic "burning its lead for safety" (`Plan C`) went from a *one-shot* bullet (`R1`) to a *zero-shot* bullet (`R8 ★`).**
   - Even against weak `Luna` (`R1`), pausing once drained Anthropic's cash so badly it had to spend the rest of the game fundraising.
   - Against **`Sol 6.1`** (`R8 ★`), OpenAI poached talent and took #1 in Turn 1 (`178.5` vs `177.7`)—so **`Opus 5.5` Anthropic paused ZERO times**, concluding that halting while behind just hands the future to less careful rivals.
3. **Domestic US bans face a double trap (`R8 ★` + `R4`).** In `R8 ★`, `Sol 6.1`'s relentless Super-PAC spending blocked federal legislation even after `Sonnet 5.5` built high voter alarm and allied with Anthropic. And on the rare roll a US-only freeze *does* pass (`R4`), compliant Anthropic stops, OpenAI/xAI moves offshore, and China takes #1.
4. **Technical reality still outweighs activist tactics by ~7x — *and `R8 ★` weakened Scott's case too*.**
   - Whether near-human AI helps solve safety or fakes safety tests dominates every sensitivity sweep.
   - **Important `R8 ★` update:** In `R1 vs R2`, blaming SURA for hurting Anthropic looked decisive (`45% → 14%`). But in `R8 ★`, **`Sonnet 5.5` played Scott's exact wish-list activist strategy** (sparing Anthropic, boycotting only OpenAI, backing Anthropic's bill) and survival *still* fell to `20.4%` (`1.0%` primary seed) because a smart OpenAI beat Anthropic anyway. **Both essays underestimate pure commercial + geopolitical race pressure.**
